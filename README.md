    # BCG Data for Decision Makers — Job Simulation

**Completed:** September 22, 2026
**Platform:** Forage
**Organisation:** Boston Consulting Group (BCG)

## Overview

This project was completed as part of the **BCG Data for Decision Makers Job Simulation on Forage**.

The simulation involved advising a hypothetical client on the performance of a multi-channel advertising campaign using simulated customer and campaign data.

## Business Problem

The objective was to analyse campaign performance across different marketing channels and customer groups and identify insights that could support better marketing decisions.

## Analysis

I analysed:

* Customer conversion trends
* Campaign performance
* Marketing channel effectiveness
* Customer and audience segments
* Campaign-channel combinations
* New customer acquisition

I used data analysis and visualisation techniques to identify patterns and translate them into business insights.

## Tools & Skills

* Power BI

* Data analysis
* Data visualisation
* Business intelligence
* KPI analysis

## Key Analysis

The analysis compared campaign and channel combinations to understand which combinations generated stronger customer acquisition results.

For example, the analysis examined whether the initial assumptions about campaign performance remained valid after incorporating channel-level results.

## Business Insights

The analysis showed that campaign performance could vary significantly depending on the marketing channel used. This highlighted the importance of evaluating campaigns at the **campaign + channel combination level**, rather than assessing campaign performance in isolation.

The findings were synthesised into a data-backed recommendation aligned with the client's marketing objectives.
